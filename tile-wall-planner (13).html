Click any tile square directly in the room preview to customize just that one square with its own photo — no need to change the whole row.

Custom colour

1) Turn on Highlighter mode and click adjacent squares to mark a run — up/down or left/right, any length — in your chosen colour. Marked squares that touch each other merge into one single space. 2) Turn Highlighter mode back off, then click that merged space once to upload a photo — it stretches across the whole marked area, like one large feature tile. Click a marked square again (with the same colour selected) to un-mark it.
